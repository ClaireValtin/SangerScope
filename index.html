References: NCBI Datasets / E-utilities (GRCh38, RefSeq, MANE). Annotation: VariantValidator, NCBI ClinVar, GeneBe, gnomAD v4, MobiDetails (REVEL, AlphaMissense, CADD, SpliceAI and more). Heterozygous SNVs are called from secondary-peak ratios; heterozygous deletions, insertions and duplications (up to 30 bp) are called by deconvolving the two-allele trace against the reference. Every call is checked against VariantValidator and must be confirmed on the trace. Sanger cannot distinguish homozygosity from hemizygosity over a deletion (allele drop-out). For laboratory use with expert review.
